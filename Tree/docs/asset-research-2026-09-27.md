# Free asset research (2026-09-27)

Checked for the four style directions. "CC0" = no attribution needed, commercial OK. Self-host fonts; no Google services at runtime.

## 1. Realistic 3D
- https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_bark_03 — CC0 8K PBR bark. Also jolcham_oak_bark_01, trident_maple_bark, pine_bark. No linden bark; oak/maple closest.
- https://polyhaven.com/a/meadow_2 — CC0 16K meadow HDRI. Siblings: meadow, evening_meadow, grasslands_sunset, sunset_meadow_path.
- https://ambientcg.com/view?id=LeafSet004 — CC0 leaf atlas (color/normal/rough/opacity). Also Grass001/004, Ground013/037, Moss.
- https://opengameart.org/content/60-cc0-vegetation-textures — CC0 cut-out plants.
- Poly Haven Namaqualand plant scans — CC0 but desert species.
- https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/linden-tree-c0c3aa73cc9945d2a36c01a0d8d6d4bb — CC BY 4.0 linden, 46k tris (reference only; we grow our own).
- Quixel Megascans: free offer ended 2024, now paid on Fab.
- https://wenrexa.itch.io/animated-butterflies — CC0, 26 butterflies x 20 frames (billboards work in 3D).

## Verdict (Simon chose realistic 3D on 2026-09-27)
- Realistic 3D: medium. Textures/skies covered; tree is ours anyway; heaviest on the phone.
- Stylized 3D: high. Everything but the hero tree is CC0 and Godot-ready.
- 2D hand-drawn: low–medium. Look is achievable with paper textures + shaders + fonts, but tree and meadow are hand art, and a 2D tree fights procedural growth.
- Pixel art: high for props/UI, but the growing linden and journal pages need drawing; also fights procedural growth.
