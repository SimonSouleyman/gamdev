# 0.8.2.7: the replay frames tonight's root; the hourglass runs at 32x and is narrower

Two notes from Simon (2026-10-02). Short test run only. Shots: `GameDev/tree-qa/0.8.2.7/`.

## 1. The camera after the run frames the root you built

Simon: "In root mode, when you have finished building the root, the camera in the replay doesn't
focus on the root you built yet. Put that much more into focus."

Before (`before_short_side/`, `before_long14/`): the settle took the bounding box of every new
node of the night, including the fine roots the night's end sprouts at old root ends anywhere in
the field, set a distance of `diagonal x 0.9 + 3` capped at 16 m, kept the low pitch and turned on
at the overview's slow drift. The first settle frame also looked at a stale point (the look point
of the pick view before the run), so the view swung away from the root at once. Measured: a 4 m
root at 16 m with old roots filling the screen; a 17 m root 9 % on screen 0.27 s in, cut at the
left edge at the end.

Now (`RootView._begin_focus`, `_fit_view`, `_update_focus`):
- **What is framed:** tonight's root and every side and fine root that grew from it (fine roots
  whose parent is an older root are left out). A night ended at once (no root of its own) frames
  all its new roots. Plus the node the root grew from, so it reads as attached.
- **How:** the same projection fit as the far view: every point inside the portrait screen with
  margins (sides 8 %, top 25 % under the vial, scrap, compass and hint, bottom 7 %), at the
  camera's own field of view and the screen's shape. Distance 4 to 45 m.
- **From where:** a high three-quarter view (pitch 0.8 rad), turned within 90 degrees of where
  the run camera looked so the whole night's growth shows largest (a small wish to stay).
- **Calm:** the orbit is first set to exactly where the run camera is and where it looks (no jump,
  no swing); then turn, pitch, distance and look point ease at 1.2/s (most of the way in about
  2 s of the settle's 4.6 s). During the settle the distance only grows, following the fine roots
  as they are drawn in (no breathing in and out). After the settle the overview's slow turn goes
  on and the frame turns with it.
- **The player's camera:** any drag (turn), wheel, pinch or trackpad zoom ends the framing; from
  then on the camera is the player's, as before. The far view cannot open while settling; after
  it, a pinch out ends the framing first, so they never fight.

Measured (`tools/grow_shot.gd --roots --run --side|--full --replay`, phone path, 450x1000, gl
compatibility, 30 fps fixed): share of tonight's nodes on screen at start / middle / end of the
settle:

| root | before | now |
|------|--------|-----|
| short (4.0 m, ended early, side roots; day 6) | 97 / 98 / 100 % at 16 m (all new nodes; root small among old ones) | 45 / 97 / 100 % at 5.9 m (root and its side roots fill the band) |
| long (17.3 m, whole tank; day 14) | 9 / 98 / 98 % at 9.4 m, cut at the edge | 22 / 95 / 100 % at 8.2 m, whole root in the band |

The "start" shot is 0.27 s in, still the run camera's view by design (no snap).

Test `tests/test_replay_0827.gd`: a short and a long root end with at least 97 % of tonight's
nodes on screen, the view turns under 4 degrees and moves under a quarter of its distance per
frame, fine roots from old ends are not framed, a drag or a zoom gives the camera back.

## 2. The hourglass runs at 32x, holding stays 16x

Simon: "When you tap the hourglass in tree mode to fast-forward, it should be twice as fast
again: 32x; hold-to-fast-forward stays at 16x."

- `TreeView.SUNSET_RUN_SPEED = 32.0` for the sunset picture; `FAST_FORWARD = 16.0` for the hold.
  Both ease in over 0.5 s, both drop to 1x for 2 s at the four moments and ease in again, and the
  run still eases out into the sunset hold.
- Same fixed sim steps (1/30 s) and the same per-frame sim budget (`main.FF_BUDGET_MS`, 12 ms;
  at most 40 steps a frame): at 60 fps 32x is about 16 steps a frame, at 30 fps about 32. A
  phone that cannot do that runs the day slower, never the frame. The tree, life force and night
  are the same (`test_a_run_day_grows_the_same_tree`, the moments tests, unchanged and passing).
- `docs/specs/fast-forward.md` updated; `test_ff_0824.gd` checks 32x for the hourglass and 16x
  for the hold.

## 4. Root mode without stick or dive button

From the coordinator (Simon's words): no "hold to dive" button, no stick drawn; a tap on an
existing root starts the night's root there, a press or drag on plain soil only turns the camera
as before; once started, a drag anywhere steers.

- **Start:** in the pick view a press on a root lights the point under the finger at once (the
  glow sphere, about a fingertip across on screen whatever the distance); the release starts
  tonight's root there. Hit test (`RootView.pick_node_at`): the nearest root *segment* on screen
  within a fingertip (`FINGER_MM` 9 mm from the screen's dpi, in the view's pixels, 40 to 140;
  about 34 px on a 96 dpi PC), then the nearer of its two nodes (nodes are 0.25 m apart). A press
  on soil lights nothing and starts nothing; a drag past 12 px turns the camera and puts the light
  out. The first night still starts at the seed when its page is read; the far view's taps on
  root ends, pinch and "let roots spread" are unchanged.
- **Steering:** `RootView._steer_input`: a finger down anywhere during the run is the centre of
  an invisible stick; the drag's offset over `STEER_RADIUS` (110 px) is the stick (right turns
  right, up turns up), lifting the finger lets go and the root keeps its heading, as before. One
  finger (the mouse, or the touch the engine passes on as the mouse), through `_unhandled_input`,
  so a tap on "let roots spread" stays the button's. The root still waits for the first drag.
  WASD and arrows still steer on a PC, space still dives; there is no dive on the phone now.
- Removed: the ThumbStick in the root HUD and the dive button (`ui/thumb_stick.gd` is unused now
  but kept). Hints and pages: "Drag anywhere to steer the root (or WASD).", "Tap a root to start
  tonight's root there. Drag to look around.", the first-night and pick pages; design doc 43/236.
- Test `tests/test_controls_0827.gd`: one button left in the root HUD and no stick; a tap on soil
  starts nothing and a drag there turns the camera; a press a little off a root lights it and the
  release starts the run from the lit node on that root; a drag right/up steers, the full
  deflection at the radius, the root grows and turns, lifting lets go and the run goes on.
- Shots: `long/pick_press.png` (pressed on a root, lit), `short/roots_run.png` (run HUD: only
  "let roots spread").
