# Surveys for Simon, 2026-10-02 (design thread)

Simon's ask after the 0.8.2.3 test: rework the journal, think about what goes into the shed's
drawers, and go through the game loop, each as a survey with proposals. Every question also has
a free-text option (Simon, 2026-10-02: he often agrees and wants to add a comment). Cards in
German, one question each; the recommended option is marked. Answers go into the specs.

## Journal

**Context (state today).** The journal is a leather notebook with six ribbons:
- **tree:** day, height, segments grown of the goal, the wish, visitors so far, and an ink sketch of the tree.
- **diary:** three one-line entries a day with a doodle, plus your own notes.
- **care:** what the tree lacks, where tonight's root finds it, the crown, the last cut.
- **clearing:** "Under my crown", the shade plants found so far.
- **pages:** the hint pages to read again.
- **settings:** the same switches as the pinboard in the shed.

On top of that, torn-out pages flutter in: hints, the "while you were away" page, the morning page.
What is irrelevant: segment counts, visitor lists every day, the settings twice, long hint texts.

**J1. Wie soll das Journal aufgebaut sein?**
- **Drei Reiter (empfohlen):** "Heute" (wish, what it lacks, where tonight), "Tagebuch", "Sammlung" (plants, finds, visitors). Settings only on the pinboard, hints at the back of the book.
- **Zwei Reiter:** only "Heute" and "Tagebuch"; collections move to the shed.
- **Wie jetzt, nur kürzer:** six ribbons stay, every text gets shorter.
- **Freitext.**

**J2. Was soll auf der Seite "Heute" stehen?**
- **Nur was das Spiel ändert (empfohlen):** the wish, what the tree lacks, where to dig tonight. Everything in at most three lines with the coloured dots.
- **Dazu Zahlen:** additionally day, height and how far to finishing (as a small bar).
- **Fast nur Bild:** a tree sketch with marks, hardly any text.
- **Freitext.**

**J3. Wie soll das Tagebuch schreiben?**
- **Nur Besonderes (empfohlen):** one line a day, only when something happened (visitor, find, wish reached, first blossom), with a doodle; quiet days stay empty.
- **Wie jetzt:** up to three lines every day.
- **Nur eigene Notizen:** the game only sets small stamps (doodles), you write the text.
- **Freitext.**

**J4. Wie sollen Hinweise erscheinen?**
- **Kurz im Bild (empfohlen):** one sentence in ink at the edge of the screen, gone on the next tap; the full page in the back of the book.
- **Abgerissene Seiten wie jetzt,** but at most two sentences.
- **Freitext.**

## Shed drawers

**Context.** If the chosen shed view still shows the workbench drawers, they should open (Simon).
What is inside should matter for the game loop, calm, nothing to buy.

**D1. Was soll in die Schubladen?**
- **Fundstücke (empfohlen):** what the roots find underground (shards, fossils, an old map scrap). Some help a little: a map scrap shows a far patch for the next night.
- **Saatgut:** seed packets of every unlocked species; the next tree is chosen here.
- **Wurzelkarten:** ink drawings of every finished tree's root network, to flip through (0.8.3 plan).
- **Freitext.**

**D2. Sollen Fundstücke einen Nutzen haben?**
- **Kleiner Hinweis (empfohlen):** at most one find a week shows something (a far patch, a rock gap); never a bonus to growth.
- **Nur Sammlung:** a drawing and a line, nothing else.
- **Freitext.**

## Game loop

**Context (state today).**
- **Day (2 to 5 min):** the sun grows the tree; a tap boosts (faster growth, less life force for the night); holding fast-forwards (16x from 0.8.2.4, plus the sunset button); the shears prune; the wish glows; visitors come.
- **Sunset:** swipe up to the roots.
- **Night (20 to 44 s):** one root. Steer it to dots (water, N, P, K), each metre costs life force. Stopping early grows small roots instead, and a full drive makes the root thicker.
- **Next day:** the scarcest nutrient sets the pace.
- **Month:** a tree finishes in about 30 days, gets an album page, and its seed unlocks the next of six species.
- **Beside it:** the bonsai in the shed, with no pressure.
- **What the sims show:** steering gains about 6 days a month, and constant boosting gains at most about 15 %.
- **Open:** with fast-forward and the sunset button the day can be skipped, so what is the day for?

**G1. Wie lang soll eine Sitzung am Tag sein?**
- **3 bis 5 Minuten (empfohlen):** a short look by day, then the night; the day may be fast-forwarded.
- **Unter 2 Minuten:** the day mostly skipped, the night is the game.
- **10 Minuten und mehr:** the day gets more to do.
- **Freitext.**

**G2. Was soll den Tag lohnend machen?**
- **Momente zu Tageszeiten (empfohlen):** visitors, the wish and weather come at set times, so watching shows something; skipping loses nothing.
- **Morgens ein Plan:** at dawn choose a focus (height, crown or roots) that steers the day's growth.
- **Mehr Pflege:** the crown needs pruning more often, and care cues come more often.
- **Freitext.**

**G3. Soll man spüren, was die Nacht gebracht hat?**
- **Morgens zeigen (empfohlen):** at dawn a short glow along the new roots and a burst of growth from what they drank; the diary names the best find.
- **Wie jetzt:** it only shows in the growth over the day.
- **Freitext.**

**G4. Was kommt nach den sechs Arten?**
- **Sammeln (empfohlen):** a field guide of the species and the finds in the drawers; trees keep coming, with rarer quirks.
- **Ein Hain:** the finished trees stand in 3D around the clearing (costs phone performance).
- **Jahreszeiten:** winter and spring change how a tree grows.
- **Freitext.**
