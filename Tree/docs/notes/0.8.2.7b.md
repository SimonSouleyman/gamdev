# 0.8.2.7b: diary gaps, bonsai mode clean-up, the shed's bench, pinboard and night lamp

Branch s0827b from main 0.8.2.6. Shots in `GameDev/tree-qa/0.8.2.7b/`.

## Simon's notes
1. **Diary without quiet days.** `Journal.shown_days(diary)`: only days with a line (`Diary.page`)
   or a note of the player's own show, newest first; a quiet day has no heading at all (it used
   to show its date). An empty diary says "Nothing to write down yet." Shot: `diary_gaps/`
   (`tools/journal_shot.gd --quiet=3,4,5,9,10` drops those days' special lines).
2. **No "back to the bench" note** in bonsai mode (`ui/bonsai_hud.gd`). A tap below the sill is
   the way back (unchanged); the hint still says so. The pellet slip may now sit higher on the left.
3. **Sharp album card** (`shed/bonsai_tools.gd`): the card's paper and sketch get mipmaps
   (`BonsaiTools.sharp_texture`, the imported pictures had none), anisotropic filtering, and the
   ink drawings are shrunk once and their lines made bolder (`ink` width) so they do not fade in
   the small mipmaps; the sketch blends instead of an alpha cut (which thinned the lines away).
   "my bonsai" is set at 128 px with a mipmapped font copy (`sharp_font`).
4. **The style picture hangs flat on the casing**: upright, its back on the casing's face
   (z 0.039, the casing at WALL_Z 0.04), no tilt; cream pages with the two drawings in ink, a nail
   above and a twine from its top corners over the nail.
5. **No name labels on the tools** (`BonsaiHud.UNNAMED`: can, tin, shears, tweezers, wire, trowel),
   first time or "clearer print" alike; a tool in hand still shows "put back" at its place (needed
   to put it down). Picking a tool up also counts as using it (`seen_pages`). The album card,
   style picture, cuttings and the turn arrows keep theirs. Hints and the status scrap unchanged.

## From the coordinator
- **Pinboard menu** (`ui/shed_menu.gd`): cork like the board on the door: a seamless generated cork
  tile (simplex mottling and crumbs) repeated in a TextureRect, inside a dark wood frame with grain
  along each side and mitred corners (nine-slice). Notes, switches and tap targets unchanged.
  Shots: `pinboard_before_*` / `pinboard_after_*` at 450x1000 and 450x800 (`shed_options*`).
- **Bench things** (`shed/shed.gd`): spread round the middle of the visible table (journal and
  gloves in front, album and seed bag behind), each set down so its lowest point lies on the
  measured table top (`table_top()`, 0.830; they were placed at 0.82, 1 cm into it).
  `tests/test_shed.gd` checks the rest (±2 mm), no overlapping bounds, the screen middle and taps
  of at least 9 mm at 450x1000 and 450x800. Shots: `shed_before_*` / `shed_after_*`.
- **The stick behind the seed bag** was the bench's decorative trowel standing on its end (its
  turn stood it up). It is gone (the bonsai's sill has its own). The bag lost its crease normal map
  (triplanar, black blotches on its sides). Close-ups from four sides: `seedbag/before_grid.png`,
  `seedbag/after_grid.png` (`tools/seedbag_shot.gd`).
- **Night lamp left of the door** (`Shed.door_lamp`): a second enamel lamp like the bonsai's on the
  front wall, off-screen to the left, a wide warm cone on the door, the pinboard and the bench's
  left half; night only, driven with the bonsai lamp. Shots: `shed_*_450x1000/shed_night.png`.
- **Green circles on the leaves** (bonsai mode): seen. They were the pads' dark "cores" (smooth
  ellipsoids inside each foliage pad) showing through the gaps between the leaf cards; they hid
  while the shears or the wire were in hand and came back when put down. `BonsaiView.SHOW_CORES`
  is false now. Shots: `green_circles_before.png`, `green_circles_after.png`,
  `green_circles_zoom_before_after.png` (`tools/shed_shot.gd --tap-tree`).
